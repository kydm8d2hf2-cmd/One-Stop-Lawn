One Stop Lawn — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 28, 2026

Thank you for using One Stop Lawn (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web application. Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Lawn Profile Information: lawn name, square footage, ZIP code, USDA hardiness zone, grass type, soil type, and address (used for satellite-imagery measurement and weather lookups).
  • Journal Entries: dates, notes, photos, fertilizer/treatment products applied (including product name, active ingredient, NPK, application rate), mowing height, watering events, and any other activity you log.
  • Soil Test Results: pH, macronutrient (N, P, K) and micronutrient (Ca, Mg, etc.) values, and rating labels (Low, Sufficient, High, Excessive).
  • Photos: lawn photos, weed/insect/fungus identification photos, soil test report scans, and product label photos. Photos are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly send them to the AI feature for analysis.
  • Subscription Selection: chosen tier (Lawn Home, Plus, Premium, Pro, Enterprise), billing period (monthly or annual), and trial status.
  • Lawn Measurements: GPS-derived polygons you draw on the satellite imagery.
  • Optional Connector Credentials: Rachio API key (only if you connect a Rachio Smart Sprinkler Controller).
  • Feedback: any text you submit through the in-app Feedback button (this opens a Tally.so form, which masks your real email — see Section 4.4).

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Approximate Location: your ZIP code (entered by you) is used to determine USDA hardiness zone and to fetch local weather. If you grant explicit permission, the App may briefly request precise GPS coordinates to drop a pin on the map for measurement; coordinates are not stored on our servers.
  • Device Information: operating system, browser, screen size, and locale (used to render the app correctly).
  • Usage State: which tab you last viewed, your dark/light mode preference, font-size preference, and other UI state stored locally on your device.

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, payment card details, government identifiers, contacts, calendar, microphone audio, or precise long-term location history. Your account does not have a server-side identity tied to you personally.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Primary storage is local. Almost all data — your lawn profile, journal entries, soil tests, photos, drawn map segments, Rachio configuration, subscription state, and all UI preferences — is stored in your device’s local storage. This data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up or share it.

Manual export/import: you can export your data as a JSON file from Settings → Export/Import, and import it back into another device. We never see this file unless you choose to send it to us.

Future cloud backup: native iOS and Android builds may offer optional cloud backup via Apple iCloud (CloudKit Private Database) or Google Drive (appDataFolder scope). These backups are end-to-end controlled by Apple/Google and only restorable to the same Apple ID or Google account that created them. We do not have access to these backups.

3. Information We Process Through Servers

The following limited interactions involve transmission of data through Anthropic-operated infrastructure or third-party services. We do not run our own data servers.

3.1 AI Lawn Care Assistant (“Ask AI”)

When you submit a question to the in-app AI assistant: - Your question text is sent to a Vercel-hosted serverless proxy (one-stop-lawn.vercel.app/api/ask), which forwards the request to Anthropic’s Claude API. - The proxy attaches an Anthropic API key (held only server-side; never exposed to your device) and may include a small set of context fields you have chosen to share — typically grass type, ZIP, hardiness zone, square footage, recent journal entries, and recent soil test results. This context is sent only when relevant to the question type. - If you attach a photo for AI analysis (e.g., weed identification), the image bytes are transmitted to Anthropic for the duration of the request. - Anthropic’s data handling for API requests is governed by Anthropic’s commercial terms; per their public policy, API inputs and outputs are not used to train their models. - Vercel may log the request URL, IP address, and timestamp at the proxy edge for the purpose of operating the service. These logs are retained per Vercel’s standard retention policy.

3.2 Rachio Smart Sprinkler Sync (Optional)

Available on Lawn Premium and higher tiers.

If you choose to connect a Rachio account: - Your Rachio API key is stored locally on your device only. - The App calls a Vercel-hosted proxy (one-stop-lawn.vercel.app/api/rachio), which forwards authenticated requests to Rachio’s Public API. - The proxy fetches your device list, zone configuration, and recent watering events. These are returned to your device and stored locally. - We never store your Rachio API key on our servers; it is forwarded in the Authorization header for each individual call and is not retained. - You can disable this at any time by removing the API key from Settings.

3.3 Address & Geocoding

When you search for a lawn address or look up your hardiness zone: - Address strings are sent to Nominatim (operated by OpenStreetMap Foundation, openstreetmap.org) for forward geocoding to latitude/longitude. - ZIP codes are sent to phzmapi.org to resolve USDA hardiness zone. - Per OSMF’s policy, Nominatim may temporarily log your IP address and the query for the purpose of operating the service.

3.4 Weather Forecasts

The App fetches public weather data (temperature, precipitation, hourly forecast) from public weather APIs based on your ZIP or coordinates. These calls do not transmit any personal information beyond the location query.

3.5 Satellite & Map Imagery

When you use the Measure Lawn feature, satellite tiles are served by OpenStreetMap-compatible tile providers. Your IP address is visible to those providers as part of the standard HTTP request.

3.6 Feedback Form

The Settings → Feedback button opens a Tally.so form. Tally collects your message and any contact info you choose to provide and forwards it to our inbox. Tally masks your real email from us and applies their own privacy policy (tally.so/privacy). Submitting feedback is optional.

3.7 GitHub-Hosted Image Assets

Some weed, fungus, and insect identification reference images are hosted on github.com/raw.githubusercontent.com. Loading these images from the Diagnose tab makes a standard HTTP request to GitHub, exposing your IP address to GitHub’s edge network.

3.8 Web Demo Mode (GitHub Pages)

If you use the public web demo at the GitHub Pages URL rather than the native app, you should be aware that: - The demo intentionally has the AI assistant and Rachio sync disabled for cost reasons. - All other data flows above still apply. - We have no analytics or tracking pixels on the demo page.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To render your lawn, journal, and reports.
  • To compute fertilizer rates, watering recommendations, and seasonal task lists tailored to your grass type and climate zone.
  • To send your questions to the AI assistant when you tap Ask AI.
  • To pull and display Rachio watering events when you connect a Rachio controller.
  • To resolve addresses, weather, and hardiness zones based on your ZIP code.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, marketing email, model training, or any other purpose.

5. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Subscriptions are processed exclusively by Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android). When you purchase or change a subscription: - You provide payment details directly to Apple or Google. We never see your payment card details or billing address. - Apple/Google return us an opaque receipt confirming the active tier and expiry date. - We use RevenueCat to validate receipts and manage subscription state. RevenueCat may receive an anonymized device identifier and the receipt; their privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. - Promotional codes (Apple Offer Codes, Apple Promo Codes, Google Play Promo Codes) are redeemed through Apple/Google’s native flows. We do not see redemption details beyond the resulting subscription state.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

We may disclose information only in the following limited cases: - Service Providers: as described in Section 3 (Anthropic for AI, Rachio for sprinkler sync, Nominatim/OSMF for geocoding, Vercel for proxy hosting, Tally.so for feedback, GitHub for asset hosting, RevenueCat for receipt validation, Apple/Google for billing). - Legal Requirements: if required by law, regulation, court order, or governmental request, we may comply with appropriate legal process. - Safety: to protect the rights, property, or safety of the App, our users, or the public. - Business Transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (you would be notified in advance and given the option to delete your data).

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and export: Settings → Export/Import → Full Backup downloads a JSON file containing all your local data.
  • Delete a lawn: Settings → My Lawns → ✕ Delete. Choose Delete All to permanently erase the lawn and all its data, or Delete Lawn Keep Data to soft-delete (data is preserved and can be restored later).
  • Delete everything: clear browser/app data, or use Settings → Export/Import → Reset All Data.
  • Opt out of AI: simply don’t use the Ask AI feature. No data is sent to Anthropic except when you explicitly submit a query.
  • Opt out of Rachio: don’t enter a Rachio API key, or remove it from Settings at any time.
  • Disable individual zones from logging: Settings → My Lawns → Sync Account → toggle individual Rachio zones Off to stop them from auto-logging.
  • Opt out of cloud backup (native apps): toggle CloudKit/Google Drive backup off in Settings.

If you reside in a jurisdiction that grants you additional rights (e.g., EU GDPR, California CCPA), you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal information. Because we store almost no personal information server-side, in most cases these requests can be fulfilled directly through the in-app controls above.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, please contact us using the address in Section 12 and we will delete any associated data.

9. Data Security

  • All network requests use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Anthropic API keys and Rachio API keys are held server-side at Vercel only; they are never embedded in client builds in plaintext.
  • Native builds use a shared-secret header (X-App-Secret) to authenticate to the Vercel proxy. The shared secret is embedded in the native binary.
  • Local storage on your device is protected by your device’s standard security model (passcode, biometric unlock, OS sandbox).

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If a security incident affecting your data occurs, we will notify affected users and any required regulators consistent with applicable law.

10. Data Retention

  • On your device: data is retained until you delete it through the App or clear app/browser data.
  • AI proxy logs (Vercel): standard Vercel edge logs are retained per Vercel’s published retention policy.
  • Anthropic API logs: governed by Anthropic’s commercial terms.
  • Rachio API logs: governed by Rachio’s privacy policy.
  • Subscription receipts: retained as long as required by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, and applicable tax/financial regulations.

11. International Users

The App is operated from the United States. If you access the App from outside the US, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the US or in regions where Anthropic, Vercel, RevenueCat, or other service providers operate (typically US and EU). By using the App, you consent to these transfers.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective Date” at the top of this policy will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice, and your continued use of the App after the change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
